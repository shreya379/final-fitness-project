How the game works

Each mission can earn XP once per day. Step logging adds a small check-in bonus the first time you save steps that day. Activity estimates are approximate. You can change or uncheck a mission on the same day; the app recalculates your XP so you don’t earn duplicate points. Missed days don’t erase your lifetime XP or badges—your streak simply reflects consecutive days with at least one completed mission.
